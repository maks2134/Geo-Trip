## 1. Theme & Executive Vision

### Theme: *"The Field Companion & Smart Expedition — Offline Dossier, Multi-Currency Ledger, Live Navigation & Travel Milestones"*

Following `v0.2.0`'s foundation of multilingual UI, system theme synchronization, MinIO media uploads, and satellite basemaps, **`v0.3.0`** focuses squarely on the active travel experience—when nomads and explorers are on the ground, navigating unfamiliar cities, managing multi-currency expenses, optimizing complex day itineraries, and operating reliably even offline or with battery constraints.

### Core Strategic Pillars:
1. **Live Navigation & Transit Matrix (`FEAT-01 / CAND-02`)**: 1-tap deep linking from stops and route legs directly into native navigation engines (Google Maps, Apple Maps, Yandex Maps).
2. **Frictionless Map Capture (`FEAT-02 / CAND-10`)**: Quick Pin drop directly on the MapLibre canvas with automatic Nominatim reverse geocoding into place name, city, and country.
3. **Multi-Currency Travel Ledger (`FEAT-03 / CAND-01`)**: Multi-currency expense tracking with dynamic exchange rate conversion to trip base currency and Redis-backed FX rate caching.
4. **Day Route Optimization (`FEAT-04 / CAND-08`)**: 2-opt TSP route sorting for day itineraries with anchor locks (hotel start / dinner finish) and instant distance savings calculation.
5. **Printable Offline Dossier (`FEAT-05 / CAND-03`)**: Zero-dependency 1-Page/Day clean PDF/print export with per-stop coordinate QR codes for offline scanning and immigration readiness.
6. **Scratch Milestones & Regional Badges (`FEAT-06 / CAND-04`)**: UN Geoscheme regional completion percentages and milestone achievement badges on the World Scratch Map.

## 3. Selected In-Scope Features (Full BA Specifications & ACs)

### FEAT-01 (CAND-02): Day Route Navigation Deep Links & Interactive Transit Matrix

#### 1. Problem / Job-to-be-Done
When travelers are in the field moving between stops on an active day itinerary, they need to quickly launch turn-by-turn navigation in their native GPS app (Google Maps on Web/Android, Apple Maps on iOS, Yandex Maps in CIS/Russia) and review walking vs. driving transit estimates between stops without manual address copying.

#### 2. User Stories
- **US-01.1**: *As a traveler walking between stops in Day 2*, I want a 1-tap "Navigate" button on any stop card that deep-links directly into Google Maps, Apple Maps, or Yandex Maps with pre-filled destination coordinates.
- **US-01.2**: *As an international nomad*, I want to configure my preferred navigation app in settings or choose from a floating action sheet.
- **US-01.3**: *As a planner reviewing a day route*, I want an interactive transit connector badge between consecutive stops showing walk vs. drive estimates and a button to launch turn-by-turn route directions between Stop A and Stop B.

#### 3. Acceptance Criteria (`Given / When / Then`)

##### Scenario 1.1: Direct deep-link navigation launch
- **Given** an ordered stop with valid coordinates `(lat, lng)`,
- **When** the user clicks the "Navigate" glass icon on the stop card,
- **Then** the application launches the user's preferred navigation app using OS-specific URI schemes:
  - **Apple Maps** (iOS/macOS): `maps://?daddr={lat},{lng}&dirflg=d`
  - **Google Maps** (Android/Web): `https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination={lat},{lng}`
  - **Yandex Maps** (CIS/Russia): `yandexmaps://maps.yandex.ru/?rtext=~{lat}%2C{lng}&rtt=auto` (with fallback to `https://yandex.com/maps/?rtext=~{lat}%2C{lng}`)

##### Scenario 1.2: Turn-by-turn leg route between Stop i and Stop i+1
- **Given** two consecutive stops A (`lat_A, lng_A`) and B (`lat_B, lng_B`) on a Day itinerary,
- **When** the user clicks the transit connector badge between the stops,
- **Then** a glass popup opens with navigation links: Google Maps (`origin={lat_A},{lng_A}&destination={lat_B},{lng_B}`), Apple Maps (`saddr={lat_A},{lng_A}&daddr={lat_B},{lng_B}`), and Yandex Maps (`rtext={lat_A}%2C{lng_A}~{lat_B}%2C{lng_B}`).

##### Scenario 1.3: Preference persistence
- **Given** the navigation app selector in user settings or action sheet,
- **When** the user chooses `Google Maps`, `Apple Maps`, or `Yandex Maps`,
- **Then** `localStorage.getItem('atlasflow_nav_app')` stores the choice and applies it to all subsequent navigation taps.

#### 4. Scope & Service Impact
- **`apps/web`**: Transit connector popover component, deep-link URI generator with device platform detection (`navigator.userAgent`), navigation preference selector in settings dock.
- **Effort**: `S` (ICE: 22.5)

### FEAT-02 (CAND-10): Quick Pin Map-Click & Auto-Reverse Geocode

#### 1. Problem / Job-to-be-Done
Travelers exploring the map frequently spot scenic viewpoints, beaches, trailheads, or cafes and want to drop a pin immediately without typing an address into a search bar.

#### 2. User Stories
- **US-02.1**: *As an explorer*, I want to click a "Drop Pin" tool and click anywhere on the MapLibre canvas to drop a marker at those exact coordinates.
- **US-02.2**: *As a user dropping a pin*, I want the system to automatically reverse-geocode the clicked coordinates into a place name, city, and country.
- **US-02.3**: *As a trip planner*, I want the Quick Pin drawer to provide a dropdown to immediately assign the pin to Backlog or a specific Day.

#### 3. Acceptance Criteria (`Given / When / Then`)

##### Scenario 2.1: Map canvas click & immediate visual feedback
- **Given** the map canvas in World or Trip mode,
- **When** the user toggles the "Quick Pin" tool in the dock and clicks at coordinates `(lat, lng)`,
- **Then** an animated pulsing pin preview appears immediately at the exact click location,
- **And** the Place Drawer slides open in "Create Place" mode with latitude and longitude pre-populated.

##### Scenario 2.2: Async reverse geocoding with graceful fallback
- **Given** a clicked coordinate `(lat, lng)`,
- **When** `geo-places.ReverseGeocode(lat, lng)` is invoked,
- **Then** within 800ms the drawer title pre-fills with the closest landmark / road / locality name, country code, and city,
- **And** if geocoding times out or returns empty (e.g. open ocean or remote mountain), the title defaults gracefully to localized `"Dropped Pin (lat, lng)"` allowing the user to type a custom name.

##### Scenario 2.3: One-click assignment to Day or Backlog
- **Given** the active trip workspace with days,
- **When** the user saves the dropped pin with a selected Day,
- **Then** `trip-core` creates the place ref with a generated Lexorank appending it to that day, and the map updates with the numbered pin.

#### 4. Scope & Service Impact
- **`apps/web`**: Map click handler in `MapCanvas`, pulsing pin layer, quick-add drawer state.
- **`services/geo-places`**: `ReverseGeocode(lat, lng)` RPC calling Nominatim reverse API with 24h caching.
- **`proto/`**: `ReverseGeocodeRequest` / `ReverseGeocodeResponse` contracts.
- **Effort**: `S` (ICE: 18.0)

### FEAT-03 (CAND-01): Multi-Currency Trip Expenses & Dynamic Base FX Conversion

#### 1. Problem / Job-to-be-Done
International travel spans multiple currencies (e.g. flights in USD, hotels in EUR, street food in THB, trains in JPY). Travelers need to record expenses in local currency while seeing the converted sum in their trip base currency with reliable exchange rates.

#### 2. User Stories
- **US-03.1**: *As a nomad traveling in Japan and Thailand with a USD trip budget*, I want to record an expense of `¥4,500 JPY` or `฿650 THB` and have it automatically converted to USD based on the daily exchange rate.
- **US-03.2**: *As a budget-conscious traveler*, I want to override the exchange rate manually if I exchanged cash at a specific rate.
- **US-03.3**: *As a user reviewing the budget tab*, I want to see both the foreign currency total and the normalized base currency total and category breakdown.

#### 3. Acceptance Criteria (`Given / When / Then`)

##### Scenario 3.1: Expense entry with foreign currency
- **Given** a trip with base currency `EUR`,
- **When** the user adds an expense with amount `15,000`, currency `JPY`, and category `food`,
- **Then** `trip-core` calculates `base_amount = amount * fx_rate` (e.g. `92.40 EUR`), stores `original_amount: 15000`, `original_currency: 'JPY'`, `fx_rate: 0.00616`, and `base_amount: 92.40`,
- **And** the Trip Budget progress bar and category breakdown update immediately in `EUR`.

##### Scenario 3.2: Dynamic FX rates provider with Redis caching
- **Given** standard currency pairs (USD, EUR, RUB, GBP, JPY, THB, TRY, GEL, AED, CHF, etc.),
- **When** exchange rates are queried,
- **Then** rates are fetched from a resilient free API (Frankfurter / Open Exchange Rates) and cached in Redis with a 12-hour TTL,
- **And** if the external rate API is unreachable, the system falls back to the last cached snapshot.

##### Scenario 3.3: Manual FX rate override
- **Given** an expense creation form,
- **When** the user toggles "Custom Exchange Rate" and enters `1 EUR = 160 JPY`,
- **Then** `base_amount` is computed using the custom rate and flagged `is_custom_rate: true`.

#### 4. Scope & Service Impact
- **`services/trip-core`**: Schema migration for `expenses` table (`original_currency`, `original_amount`, `fx_rate`, `base_amount`, `is_custom_rate`).
- **`services/gateway-auth`**: Redis FX rates cache adapter and proxy endpoint `/api/v1/fx/rates`.
- **`apps/web`**: Currency selector in ExpenseModal, dual-currency display, base currency summation in BudgetTab.
- **Effort**: `M` (ICE: 16.2)

### FEAT-04 (CAND-08): Route Optimization / TSP Sort for Day Stops (2-opt Heuristic)

#### 1. Problem / Job-to-be-Done
When travelers add multiple places to a day from their backlog or wishlist, the initial sequence is often disorganized and zig-zags across the city, wasting hours of transit time.

#### 2. User Stories
- **US-04.1**: *As a traveler planning a packed sightseeing day with 6 stops*, I want to click "Optimize Route" so the stops are reordered into the shortest logical path.
- **US-04.2**: *As a planner with a fixed start (hotel) and/or fixed end (dinner reservation)*, I want to lock the first and/or last stop during optimization.
- **US-04.3**: *As a user*, I want to see a preview of distance saved (e.g., `"Reduced from 18.4 km to 11.2 km (-39%)"`) with an "Apply" and "Undo" option before saving.

#### 3. Acceptance Criteria (`Given / When / Then`)

##### Scenario 4.1: 2-opt TSP route sorting algorithm
- **Given** a day with N ordered stops ($3 \le N \le 15$) with valid coordinates,
- **When** the user clicks "Optimize Route",
- **Then** a 2-opt heuristic computes the minimum total Haversine distance sequence in $< 50\text{ms}$,
- **And** displays a side-by-side comparison modal showing the original total km vs optimized total km and savings %.

##### Scenario 4.2: Fixed anchor points (lock first/last stop)
- **Given** an optimization request with `lock_start: true` (e.g. hotel) and/or `lock_end: true` (e.g. airport/station),
- **When** optimization runs,
- **Then** the first stop remains index 0 and/or last stop remains index N-1, while interior stops are reordered for optimal transit.

##### Scenario 4.3: Lexorank batch re-indexing on Apply
- **Given** an accepted optimization proposal,
- **When** the user clicks "Apply Optimization",
- **Then** `trip-core` updates the Lexoranks of the affected stops in a single batch mutation, the map polyline redraws smoothly, and an "Undo" toast remains active for 10 seconds.

#### 4. Scope & Service Impact
- **`services/trip-core`**: `OptimizeDayRoute` usecase implementing 2-opt TSP with fixed start/end constraints.
- **`apps/web`**: "Optimize Route" glass button in Day Timeline header, preview modal with distance comparison, optimistic update with 10s undo.
- **Effort**: `S` (ICE: 16.0)

### FEAT-05 (CAND-03): Printable & Offline Summary Dossier (1-Page/Day PDF Export with QR Codes)

#### 1. Problem / Job-to-be-Done
Travelers abroad frequently encounter battery drain, loss of cellular coverage, or border immigration checkpoints where an offline physical or PDF dossier of their itinerary, addresses, and booking vouchers is required.

#### 2. User Stories
- **US-05.1**: *As a traveler preparing for an international flight*, I want to export a print-optimized, clean 1-Page/Day PDF Dossier of my entire trip.
- **US-05.2**: *As a traveler navigating offline with a printed sheet*, I want each stop to include a small QR code linking directly to Google Maps / OpenStreetMap coordinates so I can scan it with any phone camera.
- **US-05.3**: *As a traveler passing immigration*, I want the summary to show accommodation addresses, dates, and booking voucher references clearly formatted.

#### 3. Acceptance Criteria (`Given / When / Then`)

##### Scenario 5.1: Clean print CSS and PDF generation
- **Given** an active trip with days, places, and notes,
- **When** the user clicks "Export Dossier (PDF)",
- **Then** a dedicated print-optimized view is formatted: 1 day per printed page (A4 / Letter), high-contrast monochrome/color typography, removal of interactive glass dock/chrome, and inclusion of trip title, dates, day headers, numbered stops, place categories, notes, and local phone numbers/links.

##### Scenario 5.2: Per-stop coordinate QR codes
- **Given** stops on the exported day,
- **When** the dossier is rendered,
- **Then** each stop card renders a sharp $64\times 64\text{px}$ SVG QR code containing the universal map link `https://maps.google.com/?q={lat},{lng}`, verifiable by any standard smartphone camera.

##### Scenario 5.3: Public share respect
- **Given** a guest viewing `/share/:slug`,
- **When** they click "Print / PDF Dossier",
- **Then** the generated dossier contains only public stops and notes, omitting private attachments and budget/expense tables.

#### 4. Scope & Service Impact
- **`apps/web`**: Dedicated print stylesheet (`@media print`), SVG QR code generator component (`qrcode`), 1-page/day CSS page-break rules (`page-break-after: always`).
- **Effort**: `M` (ICE: 14.4)

### FEAT-06 (CAND-04): Scratch Map Milestones & UN Geoscheme Regional Badges

#### 1. Problem / Job-to-be-Done
Scratch-map users desire gamification and visual progression. Just having a raw count of countries visited lacks milestone celebration and regional context (e.g. "You have explored 65% of Southeast Asia" or "Visited 25 countries — Silver Globe").

#### 2. User Stories
- **US-06.1**: *As a traveler scratching off countries on the World Map*, I want to see regional completion progress bars based on the UN Geoscheme (Western Europe, Southeast Asia, South America, etc.).
- **US-06.2**: *As a traveler reaching country milestones (5, 10, 25, 50, 100 countries)*, I want to unlock milestone badges ("Wanderer", "Explorer", "Globetrotter", "Centurion") with celebratory glass cards.
- **US-06.3**: *As a user*, I want to filter the scratch map by region to focus on my progress in a specific continent or subregion.

#### 3. Acceptance Criteria (`Given / When / Then`)

##### Scenario 6.1: UN Geoscheme regional completion aggregation
- **Given** a user's visited country list in `geo-places`,
- **When** `GetGeoStats` is called,
- **Then** the response aggregates stats by UN Geoscheme region and subregion (e.g. `Europe / Western Europe`: 7/9 countries (78%), `Asia / South-Eastern Asia`: 6/11 countries (55%)), returning total countries, visited count, and completion percentage.

##### Scenario 6.2: Milestone badge unlock & celebration toast
- **Given** a user visiting their 25th country,
- **When** the country is scratched as 'visited',
- **Then** a milestone celebration modal / glass badge unlocks ("Globetrotter — 25 Countries Unlocked"),
- **And** the badge is permanently showcased in the Traveler Stats glass panel.

##### Scenario 6.3: Regional map filter
- **Given** the regional selector pill in the World Map stats drawer,
- **When** a user selects "South-Eastern Asia",
- **Then** the map bounds animate (`flyTo`) to frame the selected subregion, and the country list highlights that region's countries.

#### 4. Scope & Service Impact
- **`services/geo-places`**: UN Geoscheme region taxonomy in Postgres, aggregation query in `GetGeoStats`.
- **`apps/web`**: Regional progress bars in World Stats drawer, Milestone unlock modal, region flyTo bounding boxes.
- **Effort**: `M` (ICE: 14.4)

## 5. Explicit Anti-Scope (Out of Scope)

The following capabilities are strictly forbidden from `v0.3.0` implementation:
- **NO Realtime multiplayer co-editing / WebSockets / CRDT cursors** (AtlasFlow maintains a single-owner edit model).
- **NO AI-generated itineraries or automated LLM travel agents**.
- **NO Live payment gateway integrations** (AtlasFlow is a ledger and planner, not a payment processor).
- **NO Custom vector tile hosting infrastructure** (relies on public standard vector & raster tile providers).

## 7. Technical Dependencies & Risk Controls

| Risk | Severity | Owner | Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **FX Rate API Outage or Rate Limit** | High | `go-gateway` | Cache rates in Redis with 12-hour TTL. Include static hardcoded fallback rates for major pairs (USD, EUR, RUB, JPY, GBP, THB, GEL) if external API is down. |
| **Nominatim Reverse Geocoding Rate Limits** | High | `go-geo` | Enforce client-side debounce, round coordinates to 4 decimal places (~11m accuracy) for cache keys, and cache reverse-geocoded results in Redis/memory. |
| **Print CSS Pagination Inconsistencies** | Medium | `web-map` | Enforce explicit `page-break-after: always; break-after: page;` on each day container; hide all floating glass chrome via `@media print`. |
| **TSP 2-opt Convergence on Large Days** | Medium | `go-trip` | Limit optimization to days with $\le 20$ stops; set maximum iterations to 100; guarantee $< 50\text{ms}$ execution. |
| **Public Share Privacy Leak via Dossier** | Critical | `go-gateway`, `go-trip` | Dossier generation on `/share/:slug` uses strict whitelist query omitting private attachments, expenses, and budget limits. |
